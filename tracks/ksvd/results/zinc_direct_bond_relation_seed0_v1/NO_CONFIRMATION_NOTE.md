**No follow-up compute is authorized by the completed O/T pair.** Its calibrated point gains are below the frozen `0.003` bar; the intervals include zero and also extend outside `±0.003`, and strict protocol status is `NOT_COMPLETED` due the partial-job cancellations, fresh restarts, and post-cutoff start. This note only records what evidence would be required before any *newly motivated* study could be proposed. It does not propose rerunning this contrast as-is.

## Minimum evidence needed before a new question is considered

1. A distinct scientific hypothesis, specified before accessing any new outcome data, that differentiates pair-specific bond compatibility from existing path/bond marginal information. It must identify a concrete result that would change the research decision; a different seed, coefficient, threshold, or renamed feature is not sufficient.
2. An independently designated evaluation source (new precommitted fold or other justified untouched internal evidence) and a frozen gate that accounts for training variation rather than relying only on a row bootstrap. Official valid/test remain unopened unless separately authorized by the governing research plan.
3. A protocol-compliant execution design: exactly the prespecified arms; recorded `rr` job/allocation IDs; allocation-resolved physical UUID/PCI values captured before any cancellation decision; explicit checkpoint/RNG recovery rules; both jobs scheduled before the compute stop; and a documented GPU/wall/CPU budget.
4. All scripts, masks, inputs, target constants, RNG/schedule identities, relation/beta construction, and report fields frozen and committed before formal jobs. No dev score access during training or outcome-driven changes.

Until those conditions are met and separately approved, this feature family receives **no additional compute**. A failed or uncertain result here is not a diagnosis of a general ZINC model bottleneck.
